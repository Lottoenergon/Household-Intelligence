💼 Commercial Renter & Investor Advisory
1. Building Maintenance & IPL Fee Audit
Always verify whether building maintenance charges (IPL) and sinking fund fees (typically IDR 15,000–35,000/m²/month in Jabodetabek) are bundled into the asking rent or billed separately to the tenant.
2. Model Fair Rent as Negotiation Anchor
Use the hedonic AVM valuation range as an objective baseline for counter-offers before scheduling on-site visits or negotiating with leasing brokers.
3. Furnishing & Appliance Condition Check
For units advertised as Full Furnished, inspect active condition of high-draw appliances (inverter AC, water heater, kitchen appliances) to avoid unexpected move-in maintenance costs.
4. Payment Terms & Annual Lease Discount
Full 1-year advance payments typically command an additional 5%–10% bargaining discount compared to semi-annual or quarterly payment terms.
